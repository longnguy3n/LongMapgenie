LMG v1.3.0 adds map settings to Google Drive sync and full manual backups, and blocks the ads and Admiral scripts observed on White Orchard.

- Sync includes found-location visibility, map type, interior overlays, remembered tag filters, showing notes on load and tile-coordinate swap. Presets and preset ordering remain included with their profile ownership.
- Existing map preferences are collected when you reopen a map after updating. One page collects preferences for all games on that website; open each map website you previously used before the first manual sync. Settings stay scoped to that website's origin.
- Settings, presets and progress are saved/restored together in an IndexedDB transaction. A stale tab cannot overwrite settings from a newly restored snapshot and prompts you to reload. Authentication tokens, cookies, OAuth configuration and updater paths are excluded.
- Full backups use `lmg-database-v2`. Old v1 backups remain readable and preserve settings they did not contain. Update every device to **1.3.0 or newer**; older versions reject v2 instead of discarding settings. The old Export all games format still excludes settings; use **Tải bản sao toàn bộ dữ liệu** or **Tải database hiện tại**.
- Added map-scoped network rules for identified ad providers, MapGenie's Pogo loader and the observed Admiral host. A persistent page observer hides known ad slots and late overlays branded **Powered by Admiral**, including branding inserted after the overlay. The LMG power switch controls the new rules.

Verification: 33 automated tests; isolated Chromium tests for settings capture/deletion, restoration across supported origins, stale-tab protection, presets/order, legacy backup compatibility, manual exports and profile operations. A real v1.2.2 test installation retained its progress, profiles, presets and ordering through the IndexedDB upgrade and imported its existing localStorage preferences after reload. The Windows folder-swap test also retained OAuth and IndexedDB while reloading the extension.

On the live White Orchard page, Chromium blocked the Pogo, GPT and observed Admiral script requests while the map canvas still loaded. Admiral overlay fallback was tested with fixtures; ad domains and markup may change. Live Google sign-in and transfers between two real Google Drive clients have not been validated.

Update using **Cập nhật ngay**, or extract **LMG-1.3.0-chrome.zip** and reload LMG in `chrome://extensions`. Refresh map tabs on every machine. On the source machine, reopen the maps and click **Đồng bộ ngay**; on the other machine, sync using the same Google account and OAuth project, then refresh the maps. If both sides have changes, choose the complete snapshot you want to keep.
